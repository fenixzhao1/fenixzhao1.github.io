This paper observes information acquisition before trade in a two-asset experimental market. Subjects choose asset-specific signal precision and then trade after a permanent variance shock to one asset. The shock primarily affects attention, pricing errors, and volatility for the exposed asset, with little evidence of broad contagion to the unaffected asset. The design makes it possible to observe attention choices and market prices jointly.
