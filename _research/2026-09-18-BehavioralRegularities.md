This paper studies joint pricing and quantity decisions in a stochastic newsvendor environment. We document two systematic departures from the stochastic benchmark: prices respond too little when the benchmark calls for large adjustments, and the primary deviation shifts between the price and quantity dimensions as costs change. A certainty-equivalent heuristic provides a parsimonious account of these patterns and outperforms both the stochastic benchmark and a leading anchoring model.
