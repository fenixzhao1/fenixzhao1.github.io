This paper examines ambiguity in long-lived asset markets by separating ambiguity about dividend timing from ambiguity about dividend size. Compound ambiguity does not generate a large uniform shift in average prices relative to risk. Instead, its effects appear on narrower margins: timing ambiguity increases ask-side price dispersion, while payoff ambiguity increases bid-side mispricing, bid volume, and maker orders.
